# Auto Class Opener - Chrome Extension

An automated Chrome Extension that schedules online class links (Google Meet, Zoom, MS Teams, etc.) and automatically opens them in your browser when it's time for class! Para sa mga tulad ko na nakakalimutan ang online classes.

---

## Features

* **Easy Schedule Management:**
  * **Save Classes:** Add your subject name, meeting link, class time, and day of the week.
  * **View & Delete:** Easily check your saved classes and delete old ones anytime.
* **100% Automatic Opening:**
  * Runs silently in the background and opens your meeting link in a new tab as soon as your class starts.
* **Clean Dark Cyber Aesthetic:**
  * Styled with a modern, readable dark theme for a sleek look.

---

## How to Download and Install (Step-by-Step)

Follow niyo lang tong steps to install the extension:

### Step 1: Download the Extension
1. Scroll to the top of this GitHub page.
2. Click the green **`Code`** button near the top right.
3. Click **`Download ZIP`**.
4. Once downloaded, **Extract/Unzip** the `.zip` file on your computer (e.g., on your Desktop or Downloads folder).

---

### Step 2: Install it in Google Chrome
1. Open **Google Chrome**.
2. Type this in your address bar and press **Enter**:
   ```text
   chrome://extensions/
3. In the top-right corner of the page, turn ON the switch that says Developer mode.
4. Click the Load unpacked button that appears on the top-left corner.
5. Find and select the folder you extracted in Step 1 (make sure to select the main folder containing the files).

### Step 3: Pin the Extension for Easy Access
1. Click the Puzzle Piece icon 🧩 (Extensions menu) at the top-right corner of Chrome (next to your profile picture).
2. Find Auto Class Opener and click the Pin icon 📌 so it stays on your toolbar.

### How to Use
1. Click the Auto Class Opener icon on your Chrome toolbar.
2. Fill out your class details:
    - Subject: (e.g., Math 101)
    - Time: Set your class time (e.g., 08:30 AM or 13:30).
    - Day: Choose the day of the week.
    - Meeting Link: Paste your Google Meet, Zoom, or class link.
3. Click Save Schedule.

4. Buksan lang yung chrome. Pag oras na ng klase the link will open automatically.

### Privacy & Safety
- **100% Offline & Local:** All your schedules and links are saved directly inside your own browser using chrome.storage.local. None of your personal data is sent to external servers or collected by anyone.
